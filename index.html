B · LEARNING JOURNEY

Connecting the course themes

My learning journey is organised around SCST1133 themes. Each topic contributes to understanding how data moves from a real-world source to a responsible, useful outcome.

01

Foundations of Data

Build a shared understanding of data, information, context and the role of data in computing.

Foundation → vocabulary and concepts
02

Tools and the Data Landscape

Explore the kinds of tools, platforms, roles and systems that support data work.

Tools → ecosystem and industry
03

Industry and Applications

Relate course concepts to practical examples and the responsibilities of data professionals.

Concepts → real-world context
04

Data Lifecycle

Follow data through planning, acquisition, storage, processing, use, sharing, retention and disposal.

Lifecycle → connected stages
05

Acquisition and Quality

Consider where data comes from, how it is collected, and why accuracy, completeness and context matter.

Acquisition → trustworthy inputs
06

Governance, Ethics and Privacy

Think about access, ownership, security, consent, bias, responsible use and accountability.

Governance → responsible decisions
07

Emerging Areas

Investigate developing data-related technologies and consider their opportunities, limitations and risks.

Emerging areas → future learning
